What Is It?

Citizenship by Investment

Citizenship by investment (CBI) is a legal process by which a country grants citizenship to foreign nationals in exchange for a qualifying financial contribution. These contributions are carefully structured by participating governments to attract foreign capital while extending the rights and privileges of citizenship — including a passport — to investors and their immediate families.

Programmes vary by country in terms of investment type, minimum amount, processing time, and the benefits conferred. Ghafoors Immigration Lawyers provides comprehensive guidance to help you identify the right programme for your circumstances and navigate the application process from start to finish.

  • Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 130–190+ countries worldwide
  • A second passport providing long-term security for your family
  • International banking access and financial diversification
  • Tax planning advantages and expanded business opportunities
  • Dual citizenship where legally permitted by your home country
  • Ability to include spouse, children, and in some cases parents

Full Citizenship

Citizenship by Investment Programmes

These programmes grant full citizenship and a passport to qualifying investors and their families. Investment thresholds and timelines vary by programme.

Antigua and Barbuda
Caribbean

One of the Caribbean's most established CBI programmes. Applicants can contribute to the National Development Fund or invest in approved real estate. Includes spouse and dependent children.

Investment: from USD 100,000 (NDF donation)
Processing: 3–6 months
Visa-free access: 150+ countries including UK & Schengen
Austria
Europe

Austria's citizenship by exceptional contribution is a discretionary programme for individuals who make a significant economic or cultural contribution to the country. The Austrian passport is among the world's most powerful.

Investment: Bespoke — significant economic contribution required
Processing: 12–36 months
Visa-free access: 188+ countries
Dominica
Caribbean

Dominica's Economic Citizenship Programme is one of the most cost-effective options globally. A contribution to the Economic Diversification Fund or a real estate investment qualifies applicants for full citizenship.

Investment: from USD 100,000 (EDF donation)
Processing: 2–3 months
Visa-free access: 140+ countries
Egypt
North Africa

Egypt's citizenship by investment programme allows foreign nationals to obtain Egyptian citizenship through a bank deposit, Treasury bill purchase, or direct business investment, providing access to the African continent.

Investment: from USD 250,000 (deposit or T-bills)
Processing: 3–6 months
Visa-free access: 50+ countries
Grenada
Caribbean

Grenada is the only Caribbean CBI country with an E-2 Treaty Investor agreement with the United States, enabling Grenadian citizens to apply for a US E-2 visa. Investment options include the National Transformation Fund.

Investment: from USD 150,000 (NTF donation)
Processing: 4–6 months
Visa-free access: 144+ countries + US E-2 pathway
Jordan
Middle East

Jordan offers citizenship through investment in fixed bank deposits, real estate, or business investment. The programme provides a passport with access to Arab and African nations and is well-suited for regional investors.

Investment: from USD 750,000 (bank deposit)
Processing: 3–6 months
Visa-free access: 50+ countries
Malta
Europe — EU

The Malta Exceptional Investor Naturalisation (MEIN) programme grants full EU citizenship, conferring the right to live, work, and travel freely across all European Union member states. One of the most sought-after programmes in the world.

Investment: from EUR 600,000 (exceptional contribution) + property + donation
Processing: 12–36 months (residency requirement)
Visa-free access: 184+ countries including all EU states
Montenegro
Europe

Montenegro's Citizenship by Investment programme attracted significant interest due to the country's EU accession candidacy. Investment options included real estate in approved development zones and a contribution to a government fund.

Investment: from EUR 250,000 (real estate) + EUR 100,000 government fund
Processing: 6–12 months
Visa-free access: 124+ countries
North Macedonia
Europe

North Macedonia offers a citizenship-by-investment pathway through direct investment in local companies and job creation. The programme supports the country's economic development agenda and NATO membership benefits.

Investment: from EUR 400,000 (business investment)
Processing: 3–6 months
Visa-free access: 125+ countries
St Kitts and Nevis
Caribbean — Oldest Programme

Established in 1984, St Kitts and Nevis operates the world's oldest citizenship by investment programme. It maintains a strong international reputation for rigorous due diligence and a highly respected passport.

Investment: from USD 250,000 (Sustainable Island State Contribution)
Processing: 2–6 months
Visa-free access: 157+ countries including UK & Schengen
St Lucia
Caribbean

St Lucia's Citizenship by Investment Programme offers four investment options including a National Economic Fund donation, real estate, government bonds, and enterprise projects — one of the more flexible Caribbean programmes.

Investment: from USD 100,000 (NDF donation)
Processing: 2–4 months
Visa-free access: 146+ countries
Turkey
Europe / Asia

Turkey's citizenship by investment programme is one of the most popular globally, primarily driven by real estate investment. It offers fast processing, visa-free access to over 110 countries, and strategic geographic positioning.

Investment: from USD 400,000 (real estate)
Processing: 3–6 months
Visa-free access: 110+ countries
Vanuatu
Pacific

Vanuatu's Development Support Programme (DSP) is one of the fastest citizenship by investment programmes in the world, with approval possible in as little as 30–60 days. No residency requirement and a tax-friendly environment.

Investment: from USD 130,000 (single applicant)
Processing: 1–3 months (fastest available)
Visa-free access: 130+ countries
Spain
Europe — EU

Spain's Golden Visa (Residencia para Inversores) provides EU residency for qualifying investors, with a pathway to permanent residence and ultimately Spanish citizenship after meeting the relevant residency requirements.

Investment: from EUR 500,000 (financial investment)
Processing: 2–3 months
Visa-free access: EU freedom of movement + 180+ countries

Types of Investment

Citizenship by Investment Options

Most programmes offer a choice of qualifying investment routes. Each has different financial requirements, return profiles, and holding periods. We help you select the most appropriate option.

Real Estate Investment

Purchase of government-approved residential or commercial property. Many programmes allow investors to rent out the property during the holding period, generating a return on the qualifying investment. Holding periods typically range from 3–7 years.

National Development Fund Contribution

A non-refundable donation directly to the country's official government fund. The most straightforward and often most affordable route to citizenship, typically with faster processing times than other investment options.

Government Bonds & Securities

Investment in government-issued bonds or treasury securities for a minimum holding period. This route is capital-preserving as the principal is returned at maturity, making it a financially efficient pathway to citizenship.

Business & Enterprise Investment

Direct investment in a qualifying business, including new venture creation or investment in an existing enterprise. Usually requires job creation for local citizens. Suitable for entrepreneurs and business owners looking to expand internationally.

The Legal Basis

Grant of Citizenship by Basis of Investment

Citizenship obtained through investment is legally equivalent to citizenship obtained by birth or naturalisation. Passport holders enjoy the same rights and privileges as any other national of that country, including the right to pass citizenship to future generations in many cases.

All legitimate CBI programmes require applicants to pass stringent due diligence checks conducted by the national government, authorised agents, and independent third-party security firms. This process ensures the integrity of each programme and the strength of the passports they issue.

It is important to seek qualified legal advice before selecting a programme. Not all programmes are equivalent in reputation, processing quality, or long-term stability. Ghafoors Immigration Lawyers guides clients through every stage to ensure a compliant, professionally managed application.

Golden Visas & Residency

Residence by Investment Programmes

Residence by investment (also known as golden visa programmes) grants long-term residency rights to qualifying investors and their families. Many offer a pathway to permanent residence and eventual citizenship. We advise on programmes across the following destinations.

Australia

Significant Investor Visa (SIV) — from AUD 2.5M in complying investments. Pathway to permanent residence.

Austria

Residence permit via business investment or significant economic contribution. Pathway to long-term residence and citizenship.

The Bahamas

Permanent Residence Certificate via real estate investment from USD 750,000. Accelerated review for USD 1.5M+.

Cayman Islands

Global Citizen Concierge Programme (GCCP) — residence permit via qualifying investment in business or real estate.

Costa Rica

Rentista and Pensionado programmes provide legal residence for those with qualifying passive income or investment.

Cyprus

Category F Immigration Permit — permanent residence via investment in property from EUR 300,000. Fast-track processing available.

Greece

Golden Visa — residence permit via real estate or capital investment from EUR 250,000. One of Europe's most popular programmes.

Hong Kong

Capital Investment Entrant Scheme (CIES) — residence for investors placing HKD 30M+ in eligible financial assets.

Hungary

Guest Investor Visa — residence via EUR 250,000+ investment in approved real estate investment funds. EU member state.

Ireland

Immigrant Investor Programme (IIP) — residence via enterprise investment, investment fund, or philanthropic contribution from EUR 1M.

Italy

Investor Visa — residence via government bonds (EUR 2M), Italian company shares (EUR 500,000), or innovative start-up (EUR 250,000).

Latvia

Temporary Residence Permit via real estate investment from EUR 250,000 or business investment. EU member state with Schengen access.

Luxembourg

Investor Residence — via business investment or venture capital fund contribution. One of the EU's most business-friendly jurisdictions.

Malaysia

Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) — long-term social visit pass for qualifying applicants with fixed deposit and income requirements.

Malta

Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) — permanent EU residence from EUR 150,000 in government contribution and property.

Mauritius

Permanent Residence Permit via property investment under the Integrated Resort Scheme (IRS) or Real Estate Scheme (RES) from USD 375,000.

Mexico

Temporary and permanent residence options available for those investing in real estate or holding qualifying income or financial assets.

Monaco

Residence permit via proof of financial means and a bank deposit of EUR 500,000+. No income tax for Monaco residents.

Morocco

Residence permit options available through real estate investment and business establishment. Attractive lifestyle and tax benefits.

Netherlands

Residence via startup or investor visa for those establishing a business or making a significant capital investment in a Dutch enterprise.

New Zealand

Investor Visa — residence via NZD 3M+ investment over 4 years, or Active Investor Plus Visa for NZD 5M+ in acceptable investments.

Panama

Qualified Investor Programme — permanent residence via real estate (USD 300,000+), investment fund, or fixed term deposit. Fast processing.

Portugal

Golden Visa — residence via capital transfer, fund investment, or job creation. Pathway to permanent residence and citizenship after 5 years.

Serbia

Temporary residence for business investors and property owners. Serbia's EU accession candidacy makes it an increasingly attractive destination.

Singapore

Global Investor Programme (GIP) — permanent residence via SGD 2.5M+ in a new or existing business, fund, or family office. Highly respected PR pathway.

Spain

Golden Visa — long-term EU residence via qualifying financial investment. Pathway to Spanish citizenship after 10 years of qualifying residence.

Switzerland

Lump-sum taxation (forfait fiscal) — residence for high net worth individuals who do not work in Switzerland, taxed on living expenses rather than income.

Thailand

Thailand Elite Programme — long-term residence visa from THB 600,000 (approx. USD 17,000). No investment threshold; lifestyle-focused programme.

United Arab Emirates

Golden Visa — 10-year renewable residence for investors, entrepreneurs, and skilled professionals. Property investment from AED 2M qualifies.

United Kingdom

Innovator Founder and High Potential Individual visas available for entrepreneurs and high-achieving individuals. ILR after qualifying residence period.

United States

EB-5 Immigrant Investor Programme — Green Card via investment of USD 800,000 (targeted employment area) or USD 1,050,000 with job creation requirements.

Uruguay

Residence permit available through property investment or demonstration of financial means. Pathway to permanent residence and eventual citizenship.
