Helping families reunite in the UK. We handle spouse, partner, fiancé, and family visa applications with precision and care.

Overview

What Is a Partner or Family Visa?

A UK partner visa allows the spouse, civil partner, or unmarried partner of a British citizen or settled person to live and work in the UK. Depending on your relationship, you may apply for a Spouse Visa, a Civil Partner Visa, an Unmarried Partner Visa, or a Fiancé Visa.

These visas are initially granted for 30 months (2.5 years), and can be extended for a further 30 months before qualifying for Indefinite Leave to Remain (settlement) after 5 years.

Partner and family visa applications are among the most scrutinised by the Home Office — detailed evidence of the genuine relationship and financial stability are absolutely essential.

Visa Types We Handle

Spouse Visa — For married couples where one partner is a British citizen or settled in the UK.
Civil Partner Visa — For civil partnerships, with the same requirements as a spouse visa.
Unmarried Partner Visa — For couples who have lived together for at least 2 years and are in a genuine relationship.
Fiancé (K1) Visa — For those engaged to a UK citizen, allowing entry to marry within 6 months.
Requirements

Key Eligibility Criteria

You generally need to meet these requirements to qualify for a partner or family visa:

Financial Requirement — The sponsor (UK-based partner) must earn at least £29,000 gross annual income (as of 2024). This threshold is due to increase further. Alternative sources of income may also be considered.
Accommodation — The couple must have adequate accommodation available without recourse to public funds, and the property must not be overcrowded.
Genuine Relationship — The couple must be in a genuine and subsisting relationship. Evidence of cohabitation, communication, and shared life is required.
English Language — The applicant must demonstrate English language ability at A1 level (for initial entry) through an approved test or qualification.
No Public Funds — The applicant must not have any intention of accessing public funds and must be aware of this condition.
Immigration Status — The UK-based partner must be a British citizen, settled in the UK, or hold another qualifying immigration status.

Sponsor's employer letter or self-employment records
Travel history documentation
