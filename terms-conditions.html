All prices are quoted individually based on the size, design, complexity and flavour of your cake or dessert order. A quote is provided following your enquiry and is valid for 14 days. A quote does not reserve your date — your date is only secured once a booking is confirmed and a deposit is received.

Accepted payment methods and full payment details are provided when your order is confirmed. Orders will not be released for collection or delivery until payment has been received in full. For some orders, particularly larger or last-minute ones, we may require full payment upfront.

4. Changes to Your Order

We will always try to accommodate changes. Minor changes (such as colours, wording or small design details) can usually be made up to one week before your event. Significant changes to size, structure or design may affect the price and cannot be guaranteed close to your event date. Please raise any changes with us as early as possible.

5. Cancellations

If you need to cancel, please tell us as soon as possible. Booking deposits are non-refundable, as they reserve your date and cover time and materials already committed. Cancellations made within two weeks of the event date may be charged in full, as ingredients and preparation will already be under way.
