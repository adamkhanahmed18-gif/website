What we do

A finish for every surface.

Internal Plastering

Glass-smooth skims and re-skims over tired or patched walls and ceilings — the perfect canvas for paint.

External Rendering

Weatherproof sand & cement, monocouche and silicone renders that protect and transform façades.

Lime Plastering

Breathable lime plaster and render for period and traditional buildings — the right finish for older walls.

Venetian Polished Plaster

Marble-effect Italian finishes, hand-burnished to a deep lustre for standout feature walls.

New Builds & Renovations

Full plastering for new build properties and whole-house renovations — start to finish, every wall and ceiling.

Repairs & Small Works

Cracks, holes, water damage and patch repairs put right and blended in — no job too small.
