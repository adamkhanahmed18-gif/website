Ordering & Process

Simply head to our Order Now page and fill in the enquiry form with your event details, date, and any ideas you have. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to discuss everything further and put together your quote.

We recommend at least 2 weeks for occasion cakes and 6–8 weeks for wedding cakes. Weekends fill up fast — especially in peak season — so the sooner you get in touch, the better. That said, always ask even if your date is close. We occasionally have last-minute availability.

Not at all. Many of our customers come to us with just a colour, a vibe, or a single inspiration image — and we take it from there. If you have a clear vision, brilliant. If you'd like us to guide you, we love that too. That's what we're here for.

Minor changes (colours, wording, small design tweaks) are usually fine up to a reasonable point before your event. Bigger changes to size or structure need to be raised early as they can affect the quote and ingredients. We'll always be upfront with you about what's possible.

Pricing & Payment

Every cake is priced individually based on size, design complexity, and flavour. Pricing reflects the quality of ingredients and the time that goes into each handcrafted creation. Get in touch with your requirements and we'll put together a quote — there's no obligation.

Yes — once we've agreed your design and quote, a deposit is required to secure your date in our diary. This ensures your slot is held and that we can begin sourcing everything needed for your cake. Full payment details are shared during your enquiry.

We accept bank transfer and major payment methods. Full details are provided once your order is confirmed. We don't ask for full payment upfront — just a deposit to hold your date.

🚗

Delivery & Collection

Yes. We offer both collection from Bradford and delivery across Bradford and the wider West Yorkshire area. For tiered wedding cakes and larger celebration cakes, we strongly recommend delivery so the cake arrives safely and looking perfect.

We cover all of West Yorkshire including Leeds, Huddersfield, Halifax, Wakefield, and surrounding areas. For venues further afield, just ask — we'll let you know if we can accommodate your location.

All storage instructions are provided with your cake. Generally, cakes are best kept cool, away from direct sunlight, and consumed within the timeframe we advise. Buttercream cakes should be stored at room temperature unless the weather is very warm.

Dietary & Ingredients

We're happy to discuss your needs. Please mention any dietary requirements in your enquiry and we'll let you know what we can accommodate for your celebration.

We use only premium quality ingredients — real butter, fresh eggs, finest cocoa, and seasonal produce where possible. We don't cut corners. Every cake is made from scratch in Bradford, baked fresh to order, never pre-made or frozen.

Yes. Full allergen information is provided with every order. Our kitchen handles common allergens including gluten, dairy, eggs, and nuts. If you have a severe allergy, please discuss this with us at the time of enquiry so we can advise you properly.
