Garage Conversions

Convert an integral or attached garage into additional living space — an efficient, cost-effective way to gain a new room without an extension.

What we offer

If your garage is used more for storage than for cars, converting it is one of the simplest ways to gain a genuinely useful room. Because the structure is already there, garage conversions are typically quicker and more affordable than building from scratch.

We design the new space to feel fully integrated with the rest of your home — resolving insulation, damp-proofing, heating, windows and floor levels so it's warm, comfortable and right for everyday use.

  • Home office conversions
  • Utility & boot rooms
  • Bedroom & en-suite
  • Playroom or family room
  • Annex or rental studio
