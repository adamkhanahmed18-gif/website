Loft Conversions

Turn unused loft space into a functional bedroom, bathroom or home office without losing garden space or increasing your home's footprint.

What we offer

A loft conversion is often the most cost-effective way to add a whole new room — and real value — to your home, using space you already have. Many can be carried out under permitted development, which can mean no full planning application is required.

We assess your roof structure, head height and access early on, then design a conversion that maximises usable space and natural light while meeting building regulations for fire safety, insulation and staircases.

From a simple Velux conversion to a full dormer or hip-to-gable transformation, we'll recommend the option that gives you the most usable space for your budget and roof type.

  • Dormer loft conversions
  • Hip-to-gable conversions
  • Velux / roof light conversions
  • Mansard conversions
  • En-suite & bedroom designs

Is my loft suitable for conversion?

As a rough guide, you need around 2.2–2.4 metres of head height at the highest point. The roof structure (a traditional cut roof versus modern trusses), the pitch and where the stairs can go all affect what's possible — but most homes can be converted in some form.

Send us a few photos of your loft or book a consultation and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a good candidate and which type of conversion suits it best.
