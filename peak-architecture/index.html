Our Story

A Lancashire Practice Built on Quality Design

Established in 2020, Peak Architecture Ltd is a Burnley-based practice committed to bringing thoughtful, well-executed design to homeowners and businesses across Lancashire. Led by director Haseeb Aslam, we take every project from initial concept through to planning approval and beyond.
