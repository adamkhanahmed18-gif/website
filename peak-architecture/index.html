Thoughtful design
at every scale.

Founded in 2020 by Haseeb Aslam, Peak Architecture Ltd is a planning and architectural design practice based in Burnley, Lancashire. We work across the residential and commercial sectors, taking projects from the earliest sketch through to planning consent, building regulations approval, and on-site construction oversight.

We believe good architecture does not shout. It fits its site, serves its people, and improves with age. No project is too small - we bring the same focus and care to a single-storey extension as to a full commercial development.

From Brief
to Build.

01

Initial Brief

We meet to understand your site, your goals, and your budget. No obligation, no jargon - just an honest conversation about what is possible.

02

Concept Design

We develop concept drawings and refine them with you, working through options until the design is right before any submissions are made.

03

Planning Stage

We prepare and submit your planning application, handle queries from the local authority, and guide you through to a decision.

04

Construction

We produce building regulations drawings, liaise with building control, and provide site oversight to ensure the build matches the design.
