About Us

Thoughtful Design, Built Around You

A Lancashire-based architectural practice dedicated to delivering well-considered design for residential and commercial clients since 2020.

Peak Architecture: Who We Are

Peak Architecture Ltd is a Burnley-based architectural practice established in 2020 to provide accessible, high-quality architectural design across Lancashire. We work with homeowners, landlords and businesses to bring projects to life, from first idea through to planning approval and building completion.

Our work spans the full range of residential services - new build homes, single and double-storey extensions, loft conversions, garage conversions and garden rooms - as well as commercial architecture including office fit-outs and retail refurbishments.

We believe good architecture isn't a luxury. Every client deserves design that works for their life, their budget and their home. That's why we offer a free initial consultation on every project and guide you through every stage of the process clearly and honestly.

Haseeb founded Peak Architecture Ltd in 2020 with a clear purpose: to deliver high-quality architectural design that is accessible to homeowners and businesses across Lancashire.

With a hands-on approach to every project, Haseeb personally oversees each design from initial brief to planning submission, ensuring that clients receive clear communication and considered design throughout the process.

Based in Burnley, Peak Architecture is proud to serve the communities of East Lancashire - working across Burnley, Nelson, Colne, Padiham, Clitheroe, Accrington and the surrounding areas.

How We Work

Three principles guide every project we take on, from the smallest garage conversion to a full new build.

01

Clear Communication

Architecture can be complex, but it doesn't need to be confusing. We explain every stage in plain English, so you always know exactly where your project stands and what comes next.

02

Design That Works

Good design solves problems. We listen carefully to how you use your space and design around your real life, not a generic brief. Every decision has a reason, and every reason relates back to you.

03

End-to-End Support

We don't hand you drawings and disappear. We see each project through from initial concept to planning decision and building control approval, managing the process on your behalf every step of the way.

Where We Work

Peak Architecture is based in Burnley and primarily serves clients across East Lancashire. We know the local planning authorities well — Burnley Council, Pendle Council, Rossendale Council and Ribble Valley Borough Council — which means we can advise you on local planning policy from day one.

We've delivered projects in towns across the region and are happy to consider enquiries from further afield where the project suits our skills. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just get in touch.
