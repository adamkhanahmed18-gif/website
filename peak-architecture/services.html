Our Services

What We Do

From a modest home extension to a full commercial fit-out, we provide a complete architectural design and planning service tailored to your needs and budget.

We specialise in residential and commercial architecture, guiding clients through every stage of the design and planning process. All our services include a free initial consultation, and we manage your planning or building regulations application from start to finish.

What's Included in Every Service

Whether you're extending a kitchen or building a new home, every project with Peak Architecture gets the same level of service and personal attention.

Free Initial Consultation

We start every project with a no-obligation conversation to understand your goals, budget and timeline.

Measured Survey

We visit the site and take a full measured survey of your property before any design work begins.

Design Drawings

Full architectural drawings produced using CAD software, revised until you're completely happy with the design.

Application Management

We prepare and submit your planning or building regulations application and manage it through to decision.

How It Works

A straightforward process from first conversation to completed design. We guide you through every step.

01

Free Consultation

We start with a free conversation about your project - what you want to achieve, your site or property, your budget and your timeline. No obligation.

02

Design & Drawings

We produce detailed architectural drawings based on your brief, working with you to refine the design until it's exactly right before anything is submitted.

03

Planning Application

We prepare and submit your planning application to the Local Planning Authority on your behalf, responding to any queries from the council throughout the process.

04

Building Regulations

Once planning is approved, we produce building regulations drawings for your contractor, ensuring your project can be built to the approved design safely and compliantly.

Not sure where to begin? Tell us what you're thinking and we'll explain your options, talk through feasibility and give you an honest picture of what's involved.
