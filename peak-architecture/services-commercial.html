Commercial Architecture

Architectural design for commercial clients including offices, retail units, restaurants and mixed-use developments across East Lancashire.

What we offer

We work with business owners, landlords and developers to deliver commercial spaces that work hard — balancing function, brand, building regulations and budget. From a single retail unit to a mixed-use scheme, we bring the same care and clarity we give every project.

We're experienced with change-of-use applications and the additional regulations commercial projects involve, managing the process end to end so you can focus on running your business.

  • Office fit-outs & refurbishments
  • Retail & hospitality design
  • Change of use applications
  • Industrial unit conversions
  • Commercial planning advice
