New Build Homes

Full architectural design for new build residential properties, from initial feasibility through planning application and on to building regulations approval.

What we offer

Building your own home is a once-in-a-lifetime project, and the design sets the tone for everything that follows. We guide you from the very first question — is this plot viable? — through to a fully detailed set of drawings your builder can work from.

We balance ambition with practicality: orientation and natural light, energy efficiency, local planning policy and your budget, producing a home that's individual to you and built to stand the test of time.

Whether it's a single self-build, a replacement dwelling or a small development of units, we manage the planning process and the technical detail so you can focus on the exciting decisions.

  • Site feasibility assessments
  • Design concept & planning drawings
  • Planning application management
  • Building regulations drawings
  • Self-build support

How it works

Every new build homes project follows the same clear, managed path — so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

01

Feasibility

We assess the plot, local planning policy and constraints to confirm what can realistically be built.

02

Concept design

We develop the design with you, balancing your brief, the site and planning requirements.

03

Planning application

We prepare and submit a full planning application and manage it through to decision.

04

Technical & build

We produce building regulations drawings and support you and your builder on site.

Thinking about a self-build or buying a plot?

The earlier we're involved, the better. A quick feasibility review before you commit to a plot can save significant time and money — confirming whether your vision is likely to gain planning permission and what the site can realistically support.

Already have a plot or an outline consent? Bring us the details and we'll map out the route from where you are to a home you can build.

Thinking about new build homes?

Every project starts with a free, no-obligation consultation. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll talk through the possibilities, the process and the costs.
