Skilled Worker Visa

The primary route for overseas nationals to work in the UK. Navigate the points-based system with expert guidance from our IAA regulated lawyers.

Overview

What Is the Skilled Worker Visa?

The Skilled Worker visa replaced the Tier 2 (General) visa in December 2020 as part of the UK's points-based immigration system. It allows overseas nationals to come to or stay in the UK to do an eligible job with an approved employer.

The visa is initially granted for up to 5 years (or the length of your Certificate of Sponsorship + 1 month, whichever is shorter). After 5 years, you can apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain.

The system works on a points basis — you must score enough points against a combination of mandatory and tradeable characteristics including job offer, salary, and English language ability.

Points Required

Job offer from approved sponsor 20 pts
Job at required skill level (RQF 3+) 20 pts
English language at B1 level 10 pts
Salary of at least £38,700 (general) 20 pts
Minimum required 70 pts
Requirements

Key Eligibility Criteria

Job Offer — You must have a confirmed job offer from a UK employer who holds a valid Skilled Worker sponsor licence.
Eligible Occupation — The role must be on the approved list of eligible occupations (RQF level 3 or above — equivalent to A-level).
Salary Threshold — You must be paid at least £38,700 per year or the 'going rate' for your occupation — whichever is higher. Some roles have specific salary requirements. Lower thresholds apply to shortage occupations and new entrants.
Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) — Your employer must assign you a valid Certificate of Sponsorship reference number before you apply.
English Language — You must demonstrate English language ability at B1 CEFR level through an approved test, degree, or nationality.
Maintenance Funds — You must show you have enough money to support yourself (usually £1,270 in your bank account for 28 consecutive days), unless your employer certifies maintenance.
Documentation

Tuberculosis test results (certain nationalities)
Criminal record certificate (certain roles)
