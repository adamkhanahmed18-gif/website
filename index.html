Welcome to JPS Plastering

A trade learned the long way — and it shows

JPS Plastering is run by James, a Leeds plasterer who has spent the past 18 years on commercial, domestic and industrial sites. Plastering runs in the family, and the standard hasn’t dropped a generation.

We take on the full range — new build properties, renovation projects, right down to small repair works. The same care goes into a ceiling patch as a whole house.

  • Free, no-obligation quotations
  • Tidy, clean and reliable — floors sheeted, mess gone when we leave
  • Turn up when we say we will, priced fairly the first time
