Who We Are

A Passion for
Perfect Detail

Artisan Cakery began with a simple belief: that every celebration deserves a centrepiece as special as the moment itself. Based in Bradford, West Yorkshire, we've grown into a much-loved name with a community of over 10,000 followers — but our heart remains exactly the same.

Each cake is made entirely from scratch, by hand, using premium ingredients and finished with meticulous care. Whether it's an intimate birthday or a grand wedding, we treat every order as our most important.

With a 5-star food hygiene rating and full insurance, you can order with complete peace of mind.
