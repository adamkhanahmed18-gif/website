Regulation & Compliance

IAA Accredited & Legally Trained

Ghafoors Immigration Lawyers are regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA), meaning our consultants are required to comply with strict professional and ethical standards at all times. These regulations ensure that we remain fit to practise, highly competent, and fully up to date with the constantly evolving UK Immigration Rules and policy guidance.

Our team is made up of exceptionally qualified and experienced immigration professionals, many of whom are qualified solicitors and lawyers who have chosen to specialise exclusively in immigration law. This depth of expertise allows us to approach each case with precision, strategic insight, and a thorough understanding of the law.

As a result of our rigorous preparation and high professional standards, every visa application submitted to the UK Home Office is prepared to the highest possible standard, giving our clients the strongest achievable prospect of success.

Application Support

Will My UK Visa Application Be Successful?

When you instruct Ghafoors Immigration, our lawyers support you at every stage of your application. We help you gather and organise strong supporting evidence, reducing the risk of delays or refusal by presenting a clear and comprehensive application to the Home Office.

Your dedicated lawyer will prepare a detailed Letter of Representation, grounded in immigration law, case law, and Home Office guidance, clearly setting out the strengths of your case. We also complete and submit all forms on your behalf, liaising with the Home Office until a decision is made and keeping you informed throughout.
