Our Founding Story

Rooted in Bradford,
Focused on Results

Ghafoors Immigration was founded in Bradford with a single purpose: to provide the kind of immigration advice that actually makes a difference. We saw how many people in our community were navigating a complex, often overwhelming immigration system without proper support — and we knew we could do something about it.

Over the years, we've grown into one of Bradford's most trusted immigration practices, handling everything from straightforward visa applications to the most complex settlement cases. Our Bradford roots run deep, and our commitment to the community we serve is at the heart of everything we do.

As OISC regulated consultants, we hold ourselves to the highest professional and ethical standards. Our clients trust us with some of the most important decisions of their lives — and we don't take that responsibility lightly.

Regulation & Compliance

OISC Regulated — What That Means for You

☑
OISC
Regulated

The Office of the Immigration Services Commissioner

The OISC (Office of the Immigration Services Commissioner) regulates immigration advisers in the UK. All immigration advisers must be authorised by the OISC to legally charge for immigration advice.

Being OISC regulated means we are held to a strict code of professional conduct. We must demonstrate competence, maintain professional indemnity insurance, and comply with the Commissioner's code of standards.

When you choose Ghafoors Immigration, you're choosing a practice that operates within the law — protecting you from unregulated and potentially damaging advice.

⚠

Protect yourself: Only use OISC regulated advisers or solicitors for immigration advice. Using unregulated advisers is illegal and could seriously damage your immigration status. Always check credentials at gov.uk/find-immigration-adviser
