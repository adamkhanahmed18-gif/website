Our story

Four generations, one obsession: the perfect finish.

For us, plastering runs in the family — our founder learned the trade from his father, who learned it from his, with a great-grandfather on the trowel too. JPS Plastering was established in January 2015, built on more than 18 years of hands-on experience across commercial, domestic and industrial projects.

Based at Winker Green Lodge in Leeds, we've never lost that hands-on approach. The person who quotes your job is the person who plasters it. We don't cut corners, and we don't leave until you're genuinely happy.

From a single feature wall to a full new-build fit-out, every job gets the same care and the same flawless standard.
