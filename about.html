Regulation & Compliance

IAA Regulated — What That Means for You

IAA
Regulated

The Immigration Advice Authority

The IAA (Immigration Advice Authority) regulates immigration advisers in the UK. All immigration advisers must be authorised by the IAA to legally charge for immigration advice.

Being IAA regulated means we are held to a strict code of professional conduct. We must demonstrate competence, maintain professional indemnity insurance, and comply with the Commissioner's code of standards.

When you choose Ghafoors Immigration, you're choosing a practice that operates within the law — protecting you from unregulated and potentially damaging advice. Our IAA registration number is F201500982 and our Companies House registration is 09746368.

⚠

Protect yourself: Only use IAA regulated advisers or solicitors for immigration advice. Using unregulated advisers is illegal and could seriously damage your immigration status. Always check credentials at gov.uk/find-immigration-adviser
