Our story

Fifteen years, one obsession: the perfect finish.

JPS Plastering started in 2009 with a single van, a trowel and a simple belief — that a properly plastered wall is the foundation of every beautiful room. What began as one tradesman taking on local jobs in Bradford has grown into a trusted team working right across Yorkshire.

We've never lost that hands-on approach. The person who quotes your job is the person who plasters it. We don't subcontract, we don't cut corners, and we don't leave until you're genuinely happy.

From a single feature wall to a full new-build fit-out, every job gets the same care and the same flawless standard.
