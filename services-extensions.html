Home Extensions

Single and double-storey rear, side and wrap-around extensions that genuinely increase your living space and add value to your property.

What we offer

A well-designed extension is one of the most effective ways to get more from your home without the upheaval and cost of moving. Whether you're after a bright open-plan kitchen-diner, an extra bedroom or a larger living space, we design extensions that feel like a natural part of your home rather than a bolt-on.

We handle everything from the initial measured survey and concept design through to planning submission and building regulations drawings, working closely with you to balance your wishlist against your budget and the realities of your site.

Every extension is designed around how you actually live — considering light, flow, sightlines to the garden and how the new space connects to the existing rooms — so the finished result works day to day, not just on paper.

Will my extension need planning permission?

Many single-storey rear extensions fall under permitted development, meaning no full planning application is required — but the limits on size, height and boundaries are strict, and they're removed in conservation areas. Double-storey and side extensions usually do need permission.

Not sure where your project stands? We'll tell you quickly whether it's permitted development or needs an application — and we can secure a Lawful Development Certificate for written proof.
