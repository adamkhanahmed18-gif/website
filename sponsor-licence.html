Overview

What Is a Sponsor Licence?

A Sponsor Licence is a UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) authorisation that allows a UK employer to sponsor overseas nationals to work in the UK under the Skilled Worker, Health and Care Worker, or other sponsored work routes. Without a valid licence, you cannot issue a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) — and without a CoS, your overseas recruit cannot apply for a visa.

Once granted, a licence is valid for 4 years and must be renewed. Holding a licence also brings ongoing compliance obligations — you must maintain accurate records, report certain changes to UKVI, and cooperate with Home Office compliance visits.

Getting the application right from the start — particularly around HR systems and key personnel — avoids the costly delays and compliance risks that can come from a poorly prepared submission.

Requirements

Key Eligibility Criteria

Legitimate UK Business — Your organisation must be legally operating in the UK. Sole traders and newly registered businesses can apply, but UKVI will scrutinise the genuineness of your operations.
Genuine Vacancy — You must have a real, eligible job vacancy that meets the skill level and salary requirements of the relevant sponsored worker route.
Robust HR Systems — UKVI expects you to have systems in place to track your sponsored workers, record their contact details, monitor absences, and report changes. We help you demonstrate this clearly in your application.
Key Personnel Appointed — You must designate an Authorising Officer, Key Contact, and at least one Level 1 User. These people have different responsibilities and must be named in the application.
No Unspent Criminal Convictions — Key personnel must not have unspent convictions for immigration or fraud offences. Previous licence revocations or compliance breaches will also be scrutinised.
Not Recently Insolvent or Wound Up — Organisations that have been insolvent, placed into administration, or struck off are unlikely to be granted a licence without a full explanation and supporting evidence.

Sponsor Licence FAQs

The standard processing time is approximately 8 weeks from receipt of the application and supporting documents. UKVI also offers a priority service for an additional fee of £500, which aims to process applications within 10 working days. We'll advise on which option is right for your timescales when you contact us.
As a licensed sponsor, you must: keep records of your sponsored workers (contact details, absences, salary changes); report significant changes such as a worker leaving, changing roles, or being absent without permission; carry out right-to-work checks on all employees; and cooperate with any UKVI compliance visit. Failure to meet these duties can result in your licence being downgraded, suspended, or revoked.
Yes, new businesses and sole traders can apply. However, UKVI will scrutinise applications from newer businesses more closely and may request additional evidence of genuine trading activity and the authenticity of the vacancy. We help ensure your application is as strong as possible, including compiling the right supporting evidence for newer companies.
UKVI can visit your premises — announced or unannounced — to check your compliance with sponsor duties. They will review your HR records, right-to-work checks, salary payments, and more. If issues are found, your licence can be downgraded from an A-rating to a B-rating (with an action plan), suspended pending investigation, or revoked entirely. We provide compliance audits to identify and fix vulnerabilities before a visit occurs.
Once your licence is granted, you will access the Sponsor Management System (SMS) to assign CoS to your workers. Each CoS must accurately reflect the job role, salary, and start date. Getting the CoS details right is critical — inconsistencies between the CoS and the visa application are a common cause of refusals. We advise on CoS preparation as part of our post-licence support.
