Last updated: June 2026

At Artisan Cakery ("we", "us", "our") we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or use our website, how we use it, and the rights you have. We handle your information in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who We Are

Artisan Cakery is a bespoke cake business based in Bradford, West Yorkshire. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the "data controller" of the personal information you provide to us.

Information We Collect

We only collect the information we need to respond to your enquiry and fulfil your order. This may include:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number.
  • Order details — your event date, event type, and any design ideas, flavours or dietary requirements you share with us.
  • Messages — the content of any messages you send us through our enquiry form, by phone, on WhatsApp or on social media.

We do not collect or store any card or bank payment details through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • Prepare a quote and discuss your cake or dessert order.
  • Arrange your booking, delivery or collection.
  • Keep a record of your order and communicate with you about it.

Our Lawful Basis

We rely on the following lawful bases for processing your information: consent (when you choose to contact us), taking steps to enter into a contract (preparing your quote and order), and our legitimate interests (running and improving our small business and keeping order records).

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We will never share it for marketing purposes. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us run our business — for example, the platform that hosts our website and delivers our enquiry form. These providers only process your data on our instructions. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information only for as long as necessary. Enquiry and order details are generally kept for up to two years so we can provide follow-up service and keep proper business records, after which they are securely deleted, unless we are required to keep them longer for legal or accounting reasons.

Keeping Your Information Safe

We take appropriate steps to keep your personal information secure and to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information where there is no reason for us to keep it.
  • Object to or ask us to restrict how we use your information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please get in touch using the details below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies. You can read more about these in our Cookies Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
