Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Who We Are

Ghafoors & Ghafoor Limited ("we", "us", "our") is an IAA regulated immigration practice. IAA Reference Number: F201500982. We are the data controller for the personal information we hold about you.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, you can contact us at contact@ghafoors.uk or by calling 033 0133 3687.

2. What Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal information when you interact with us:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, phone number and postal address.
  • Enquiry details — information you provide through our contact form, by email, phone or in person, including the nature of your immigration matter.
  • Immigration information — if you become a client, we will collect documentation and information necessary to handle your case, which may include passport details, visa history, nationality, family circumstances and financial records.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, pages visited and time spent on our website, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your free initial assessment.
  • To provide immigration advice and legal services if you instruct us.
  • To communicate with you about your case, including updates, requests for documentation and notifications of important deadlines.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, including identity verification and anti-money laundering checks.
  • To improve our website and services based on how visitors use our site.
  • To send you relevant information about our services where you have consented, or where we have a legitimate interest in doing so.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases for processing your data:

  • Contract — processing is necessary to provide the services you have requested or to take steps before entering a contract.
  • Legal obligation — we must process certain data to comply with our regulatory obligations as an IAA regulated firm.
  • Legitimate interests — we have a legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, improving our services and marketing to prospective clients in a proportionate way.
  • Consent — where you have given explicit consent, for example by ticking the consent box on our contact form.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) and other government bodies — as required to submit visa applications and comply with immigration law.
  • Service providers — third-party suppliers who assist us in operating our website and business (such as email and form processing services), who are bound by data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Professional advisers — solicitors, barristers or other regulated professionals where required for your case, on a strictly need-to-know basis.
  • Legal requirement — if required to do so by law, court order or regulatory authority.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this policy:

  • Enquiries that do not proceed to instruction — retained for up to 12 months, then securely deleted.
  • Client files — retained for a minimum of 6 years from the conclusion of the matter, in line with standard legal practice requirements and our regulatory obligations.
  • Website analytics data — retained in anonymised form for up to 26 months.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors interact with our website. This data is aggregated and anonymised.
  • Preference cookies — used to remember your cookie consent choice.

You can decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner when you first visit our site, or by adjusting your browser settings at any time.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — you can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — you can request that we delete your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Right to restriction — you can ask us to restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@ghafoors.uk. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

9. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction or alteration. Our staff are trained in data protection obligations and access to personal data is restricted on a need-to-know basis. In the event of a data breach that is likely to affect your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the ICO as required by law.

10. International Transfers

Where your case requires it, we may need to transfer personal data to overseas embassies, consulates or immigration authorities as part of a visa application. Such transfers are made only to the extent required by law or for the specific purpose of your matter, and are protected by the applicable international conventions and data transfer safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
