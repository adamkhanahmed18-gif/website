Second British Passport

Fast, simple, and efficient. We are an authorised agency specialising in Second British Passport applications — your original passport is not required.

The Service

What is a 2nd British Passport?

A Second British Passport allows an individual to legally hold two British passports at the same time and use both simultaneously. Each passport is issued with a different passport number and is completely separate, with no link between them.

This service is unique because many people are unaware that holding two British passports at the same time is legally possible. The application can be completed using only a photocopy of your existing passport — your original is not required. In certain cases, the process can also be completed on a same-day basis through an expedited service.

Travel whilst your other passport is having a visa application processed at an embassy.

A solution for those whose passport pages fill too quickly and wish to continue travelling stress free.

Avoid travel issues due to conflicting country immigration stamps, e.g., Israel & some Middle Eastern countries.

A second British passport carries a different passport number and is not a duplicate. It is valid for 10 years from the day of issue and can be renewed if still required.

The second passport is ready within 7 working days from the date that we attend the booked HMPO appointment on your behalf. Expedited same-day processing is available in certain cases.
